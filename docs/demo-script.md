# VoiceTask Demo Script

**Context:** "We are demonstrating VoiceTask, an AI tool that turns messy WhatsApp-style chats into organized tasks."

## Step 1: The Problem
*Show the Home screen with the empty input box.*
"Imagine you're a small business owner. You just got a frantic voice note or text from your partner that looks like this."

## Step 2: The Input
*Paste the following text into the input:*
> "Hey can you send the invoice to Rahul by tomorrow, and also book the venue for Saturday, it's urgent. Oh and order more coffee."

## Step 3: The Magic
*Click "Extract Tasks". Point out the loading state.*
"Instead of manually creating these tasks, our AI processes the text..."

## Step 4: The Result (Dashboard)
*Wait for it to load and redirect to Dashboard.*
"And just like that, we have three structured tasks:
1. Send invoice (Owner: Rahul, Due: Tomorrow, Priority: Medium)
2. Book venue (Due: Saturday, Priority: High)
3. Order coffee (Priority: Low)

Notice the color-coded priorities and the dynamic 'due soon' badges."

## Step 5: Management
*Click the circle icon to mark a task as done.*
"When a task is finished, simply mark it done. It moves to the completed section, keeping your workspace clean."
